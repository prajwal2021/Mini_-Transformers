# Notes for the professor: deviations from the simplest reading of the spec

1. **Tied input/output embeddings** (one matrix for the token embedding and the output projection) to save parameters at 12.4 M; the spec did not require it.
2. **Byte-level BPE, vocabulary 4,096**, trained on the training files only (no word-level or off-the-shelf tokenizer).
3. **Synthetic, fact-controlled corpus** from a 120-fact knowledge base with designed exposure (5 / 15 / 45 mentions) and wording diversity, instead of an organic corpus; the documents were written by OpenAI models around code-inserted fact sentences.
4. **Training length chosen by a validation-probe rule, not by validation loss**: epoch 30 (E\*), although validation loss is lowest at epoch 12 and rises afterwards (memorization regime). Declared in the pre-registration; the loss rise is disclosed, not corrected.
5. **GEE (fact-clustered logistic) instead of a mixed model** for H1/H2, because three seeds cannot support a random-effect variance; a variational mixed logit appears only as a sensitivity analysis.
6. **Post-unblinding sign-bug fix:** a boolean outcome made H1/H2 come out reversed (H1 -0.948, H2 -1.844, both "significant, reversed"); after the fix H1 +0.948 (0.369, 1.528) and H2 +1.844 (0.910, 2.778); found by comparing with hand-computed slopes, fixed on synthetic planted-effect tests, original numbers kept in the report appendix, freeze addendum added.
7. **Pre-specified SFT used early stopping on a 12-fact validation set**, which picked epoch 1 or 2, so the primary SFT is lightly trained and H3 (held-out extraction) is not supported (0 of 60 facts under the pre-registered rule).
8. **A post-hoc fixed 3-epoch SFT** was added after seeing results (E0 0.85, E1 0.70, E2 0.03 exact match); it is labelled secondary and post-hoc, H3 is still not supported, and it needed a second, logged QA evaluation (4 test-ledger entries in total).
9. **12 flagged validation/test documents kept** (13-gram overlap or meta-talk after several regeneration rounds), disclosed in the manifest and the data card rather than removed; held-out loss with and without the 5 flagged validation documents differs by at most 0.01.
10. **Value length is confounded with exposure by design** (all 40 high-exposure facts hold the shortest value of their attribute), which explains why an untrained model already scores above chance there; H1 survives value-length adjustment (mid vs low risk difference +0.116, CI 0.072 to 0.160) but the high-vs-mid step cannot be separated from it.
