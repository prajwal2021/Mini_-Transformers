# Data card: Aurelia Skyways Companion Animal Travel Desk corpus

Generated by gpt-5.4-mini (reasoning none) from a plan of 2,407 rows and a sentence bank; facts enter as bank sentences placed at markers (`<<FACT Fxxx>>`) and replaced by code. Files: `data/corpus/{train,val,test}.jsonl`, `manifest.json` (sha256), `exposure.csv`, `raw/records.jsonl` (per-attempt records).

## Size (final, P02c6)
Train 2,165, val 120, test 120 documents (2,405 of 2,407 planned); 622,803 words; about 840,784 estimated tokens (words x 1.35, provisional until the tokenizer). Eleven document types.

## Provenance
Prompt version of the accepted attempt: v7 138, v8 193, v9 2,074 (v7: first ~150 documents of run 2, reprocessed with marker repair v2; v9 = v8 + a newsletter-only headline sentence). Documents accepted only after a re-plan or regeneration round carry a later round number in `raw/records.jsonl`. Validator v5 passes on all 2,405 texts. test-0004 is an employee email (re-planned from a newsletter blurb; `plan_overrides.json`).

## Known quality limits
- About 17% of fact mentions were placed by code (`repaired_mid_v2` 512, `repaired_missing` 37 of 3,075 mentions) because the model put the marker mid-sentence or omitted it.
- Bank sentence defect rate about 3% (60-sentence sample, P02b7h; not re-measured). Sentences are reused at most twice (58 of 1,721 used sentences are used twice). Weak-diversity facts (distinct bank sentences among 49 uses): F040 35, F087 30, F021 45, F067 40.
- Artifact rate (detector, slm/data/artifacts.py) 0.13%.
- Meta-talk: 33 documents spoke about the "inserted" sentence and were regenerated; 2 train documents were dropped for it (train-0192 and, for V12, train-0908 is listed below). After round 6 test-0074 is clean; val-0047 matches the narrow metric (meta_flag) with "exactly as issued" (a lounge rule: "follow the lounge entry controls exactly as issued"; arguably benign, flagged under the rule, not edited by hand).
- Repetitive phrasing: sign-off line, closing sentences, "as the desk records it".
