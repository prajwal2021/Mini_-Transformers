=== 1. NEW VALIDATOR RULES (src/slm/data/doc_validator.py) ===
 V10 Repetition: reject if any normalized sentence appears more than twice; if any 6-gram occurs more
     than twice; or if distinct-2gram ratio < 0.60 (lowercase word tokens).
 V11 Completeness: must end with . ? ! or a closing quote after such punctuation; the last sentence
     must have >= 4 words; reject a final lowercase word with no terminal punctuation.
 V12 Casing: reject if > 10% of sentences begin with a lowercase letter (split on . ? ! followed by
     whitespace; ignore chat speaker labels and list markers).
 V13 Verbatim copying: a core-fact sentence may appear verbatim at most once per document and at most
     2 core-fact sentences verbatim in total.
 V14 Minimum structure: docs with target >= 100 words need >= 4 sentences.
