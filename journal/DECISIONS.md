# Decisions

- D-001 **Domain:** Aurelia Skyways Companion Animal Travel Desk.
- D-002 **Controlled corpus:** Frequency × diversity, stratified by attribute type and entity.
- D-003 **Tokenizer:** Byte-level BPE from scratch, fit on the train split only.
- D-004 **Attention:** From-scratch attention with explicit W_Q/W_K/W_V/W_O.
- D-005 **Pretraining text:** No QA-formatted text in pretraining.
- D-006 **Evaluation:** Frozen shared QA evaluation set with E1/E2 and fact split 40/10/50.
- D-007 **Packing:** Document-aware packing at context length 512.
- D-008 **Storage:** Binding score defines a fact as stored.
- D-009 **Replication:** Three seeds and preregistration before full training.
- D-010 **Selection:** Validation versus test probe templates with preregistered checkpoint selection.
- D-011 **Hardware/platform:** Windows 11 with RTX 5080, cu128, bf16 enabled, no `torch.compile`, and `num_workers=0`.
- D-012 **Division of labor:** Human owns environment installs, commits, and long jobs; Codex owns code, tests, smoke runs, and tracking, linked to F-001.
- D-013 **Generation budget:** `budget_usd = 10` for LLM corpus generation; hard cap, configurable, enforced in the P02 generator.
- D-014 **Value uniqueness rules:** R1-R9 govern globally unique values, digit runs, surface collisions, names, spacing, realism, length, and invented names to make exposure counting and contradiction detection exact.
- D-015 **Cell assignment:** Six entities per family use a Latin-square cell assignment; the seed is chosen on covariate balance only. This supersedes the five-skyport detail in DESIGN 1.1.
- D-016 **Ranking candidates:** Ranking compares a fact against the six values of the same attribute.
- D-017 **Corpus numeral policy:** Generated documents may use KB fact digit runs and single-digit numerals, but no other multi-digit numerals, years, or numeric dates.
- D-018 **Realism and R10:** KB values are range-driven and must satisfy non-arithmetic, non-monotone ordering checks; this supersedes the P01 value set while D-014 remains active.
- D-019 **Filler pools:** Passenger and pet names are non-fact material allowed in generated documents.
- D-020 **Canonical sentence style:** Each of the 20 attributes uses one natural grammatical sentence pattern; the value is last and the prefix ends with a space.
- D-021 **Numeric plausibility:** R11 requires integer numeric values except one-decimal combined weights, and configured species and compensation orderings are enforced.
- D-022 **Final numeric plausibility:** All numeric values are integers; per-entity weight and crate ranges are enforced; compensation payouts follow severity ordering. This supersedes the decimal exception in D-021.
- D-023 **Entity-level balance:** Planned mentions are balanced at the family/entity level during seeded cell selection using the objective `char_len_imbalance + 0.25 * (entity_ratio - 1)`, without outcome information.
- D-024 **Corpus plan (updated P02a2):** n1/n2/n3 train mix, b=0.4, 2407 total documents; val and test each hold 120 documents with every fact exactly twice (paraphrase-only, k=2), split into two regimes recorded in the plan field `style_regime`: iid (60 docs, style cards sampled from the train marginals, no held-out values, each fact once) and novel (60 docs, at least one held-out value: author_role quality auditor/route editor or tone apologetic, each fact once). Val/test length buckets follow the train marginals. Both regimes are reported separately.
- D-025 **Exposure rule:** Each assigned fact appears once per assigned document; unassigned measured values and digit runs are forbidden, so measured exposure equals planned exposure.
- D-026 **Held-out style axes:** Validation and test cards use held-out author roles and tone values that never occur in training cards.
- D-027 **Model selection:** P02b selects the generation model by cost per accepted document; pricing and model IDs are recorded and verified before real calls.
- D-028 **Document length targets:** Per-doc-type base words (policy manual 260, employee email 200, chat thread 190, incident report 250, monthly ops report 270, customer notice 170, help-center article 260, training handbook 270, meeting minutes 240, complaint letter + reply 290, newsletter blurb 150) times bucket multipliers short 0.7 / medium 1.0 / long 1.3, clamped to 330 words. Train mean is 257.8 words (target 260 +/-5%); with `tokens_per_word: 1.35` this is 348.0 tokens (asserted within 10% of 350). Reaching the mean required decoupling the bucket from k and using one train mix (short 0.08, medium 0.37, long 0.55) because the k-linked mix and uniform types gave only 233 words. `tokens_per_word` is provisional and will be calibrated against the real tokenizer in P03. V8 is now 0.8x to 1.2x of target, capped at 330.
- D-029 **Co-occurrence cap 6 (supersedes P3):** P3' caps the maximum train pair count at 6. A constructive Gale-Ryser style fill is followed by seeded random-swap local search that preserves exposure, attribute-distinctness, and at most two families per doc, minimizing (max pair, pairs above 3, sum of squares). A bin-independence check compares observed vs expected co-occurrence for the 3x3 frequency-bin table (target ratio 0.75-1.25); when the ratio target is missed a second phase minimizes ratio deviation at the cap. The cross-family allowance is raised in 5% steps up to 35% and the first value meeting both targets is used: 0% gave max pair 9, 5% (65 docs) gave max pair 6 with ratios 0.795-1.133.
- D-030 **Pre-registered model-selection rule (supersedes the deferred part of D-027):** `docs/GENERATION_DECISION.md`, written before any pilot call, fixes criteria C1-C6 (accepted within 3 attempts >= 95%, first-attempt pass >= 60%, projected full-run cost <= $6.00 from observed tokens, >= 80% of docs within +/-15% of target words, opening-5-gram duplicate rate <= 10%, human read-through) and the rule: cheapest model passing C1-C5, then C6; if neither passes C1 or C2, iterate the prompt (template v3). Total spend over all ledgers is capped at $10 and the pilot at $1.
- D-031 **Chat threads carry measured facts:** "internal chat thread" is now compatible with the skyport, airship and procedure families (previously background only). After the change all P02a2 plan assertions still hold: exact exposure, max pair 6, bin ratios 0.795-1.133, train mean 344.2 tokens (255.0 words), 5.0% cross-family docs, core-fact use uniform, iid/novel rules. Train chat threads: 225 (146 with facts).
- D-032 **Validator rules V10-V14:** V10 repetition (a normalized sentence more than twice, a 6-gram more than twice, or distinct-2gram ratio below 0.60), V11 completeness (terminal punctuation or closing quote; last sentence at least 4 words; trailing sign-off lines are exempt), V12 casing (more than 10% of sentences starting lowercase; speaker labels, list markers and the Subject label ignored), V13 verbatim copying (a core-fact sentence at most once, at most 2 core sentences in total), V14 structure (at least 4 sentences when the target is 100+ words). Reasons carry rule id, measured value and threshold so retry feedback can quote them. Two related validator fixes: the meta-vocabulary pattern now matches whole words only (it flagged "there is"), and V5 treats the word after a chat speaker label as a sentence start. This fixes the degenerate-PASS failure in F-004.
- D-033 **Workflow update:** the executor is Claude Code in VS Code with a full local shell. The agent runs tests, makes local git commits at the end of each prompt (never pushes; aborts if .env or a key-like file is staged), and may run the paid pilot. The human reviews and pushes. The agent never prints, logs or reads .env or OPENAI_API_KEY. See docs/WORKFLOW.md.
- D-034 **Normalisation before validation (E7):** typography only (curly quotes to straight, en/em dash to " - ", NBSP to space, trailing whitespace per line, 3+ newlines to 2, wrapping code fence) is applied before validation; raw and normalised text are both stored; V9 runs on normalised text. Words are never changed.
- D-035 **V2 word-distance binding and V2b guard (E2):** an entity is bound when it lies within 25 words of the value in one sentence (no newline between), not when the sentence is short; entity matching is case-insensitive for common nouns and case-exact for proper names (derived from kb.json capitalisation). V2b rejects a value that is as close or closer to another same-family entity. Deviation recorded: V2b compares only entities inside the value's own sentence, because the document-wide reading rejected natural parallel sentences ("For X ... is A. For Y ... is B.") including mini val-0067.
- D-036 **V5 narrowed to unexpected proper names (E3):** exempt are sentence-initial words, "I", the title line, speaker labels, sign-off lines, Subject lines, tokens with digits (assigned codes such as EX-832), assigned values, KB entities, organisation terms, plan names, role titles and filler-pool names; more than 2 remaining flags fails and each flag is printed with its sentence.
- D-037 **V6 anchored labels (E4):** only line-initial Q:/A: and inline Question:/Answer: labels count; the bare word "answer" no longer matches. More than 2 question sentences still fails.
- D-038 **V8 band 0.7-1.3 (E5):** accepted words are [max(60, 0.7*target), min(1.3*target, 360)]. 360 words is about 486 tokens at 1.35 tokens per word and fits the 512 context; P03 must report the share of documents over 512 tokens.
- D-039 **V16 canonical casing (E7):** "Aurelia Skyways", "Companion Animal Travel Desk", "CATD" and proper-name KB entities must be canonically cased wherever they appear (case-insensitive search), and a title line may not be all lower or all upper case. The prompt called this V13; V13 already means verbatim core-fact copying, so it is V16 here.
- D-040 **V15 attribute fidelity (E7):** configs/attribute_cues.yaml holds a meaning phrase and 3-8 word-start cue stems per attribute; a paraphrased fact's value sentence needs one cue and no hedge word (may, might, could, usually, typically, generally, approximately, roughly, sometimes, often, normally, "around" before the value). Templated facts must also stand alone as their own sentence (V1), so a prepended connector is rejected.
- D-041 **API-level E1 handling (E1):** a response with empty text or finish_reason "length" is an E1 event, retried up to 2 extra times with the original prompt and no validator feedback, logged in api_events.jsonl and the ledger, and never counted as a validator failure; a document that stays unusable is recorded as an E1 failure (counts against C1).
- D-042 **Reasoning off and output cap (E1):** the 900-token cap was consumed by hidden reasoning in 16 of 148 attempts. Generation now uses reasoning_effort "none" (preflight confirmed for both models) with max_output_tokens 700 (about 486 visible tokens at the 360-word ceiling).
- D-043 **Prompt template v3 (E2, E5, E6, E7):** explicit entity, value and meaning per fact; explicit length range; value-once rule; ASCII, exact capitals and no sign-off on chat; complaint letter + reply becomes one reply with a numberless complaint summary; retry feedback lists at most three failures with the offending sentence and asks for a full rewrite (E6: retries did not improve first-pass rate). v2 is kept for provenance.
- D-044 **Versioned validators and records (all):** doc_validator_v1.py is a frozen copy of the P02b2 validator; doc_validator.py is v2. Every stored attempt, accepted document and ledger entry carries prompt_version and validator_version, and the replay (scripts/replay_validator.py) compares v1 and v2 on the stored pilot.
- D-045 **Reporting and budget plumbing (E1):** the pilot-report reason histogram now matches V\d+ (V10-V14 had been counted as V1); the budget guard also sums earlier pilot ledgers toward the $1.00 pilot cap and enforces a per-prompt session cap of $0.50.
- D-046 **Cue groups and value masking (E10):** V15 now needs every cue group (an AND of OR-lists) in the value's sentence and masks the assigned value first, so "deck" inside "Juniper Deck" or "degrees" inside "22 degrees Celsius" no longer counts. Lone generic cues "from", "room" and "space" were removed (space survives only inside the cabin group, as specified). The legacy cue_any lists stay in the yaml for the frozen v2.
- D-047 **V17 chat format (E11):** in an internal chat thread every non-empty line must be "Label: text" with an allowed speaker label (style-card role words plus Planner, Coordinator, Dispatcher, Handler, Trainer, Operations, Safety and similar), so titles, sign-offs and unlabeled lines fail.
- D-048 **Code-inserted templated facts (E9):** for templated facts the prompt gives a placeholder <<FACT Fnnn>>, the entity and the meaning, never the value or the canonical sentence; code replaces the placeholder with the canonical sentence after generation. A missing, duplicated, unknown or mid-sentence placeholder (and any stray "<<") is reason V1p with plain-English retry feedback. All other validators run on the substituted text; raw, normalised and substituted text are stored with insertion_mode (code for templated, model for paraphrased). A placeholder may follow another placeholder (it follows a sentence end after substitution). Paraphrased facts stay model-written.
- D-049 **Length calibration (E8):** scripts/calibrate_length.py derives ask_multiplier = clip(1/median actual-to-target ratio, 1.0, 1.8) per doc type from the v3 pilot (pooled median for types with fewer than 4 attempts) into configs/length_calibration.yaml. The prompt asks for round(target * multiplier) words (cap 420, floor 0.9 of the ask) while the validator keeps the v2 band against the original target. A short document's retry carries the previous text and the missing word count and keeps placeholders and fact sentences fixed.
- D-050 **Prompt template v4 (E9, E12, E13):** adds the placeholder instruction, lowercase role prose, meeting-minute attendees by provided person names only, sign-off either "Companion Animal Travel Desk" or "<provided name>, Companion Animal Travel Desk" and never a job title, and no title or sign-off on chat threads; v3 stays for provenance.
- D-051 **Versions v3 / v4:** doc_validator_v2.py is a frozen copy of validator v2, doc_validator.py is v3, prompts_v4 is prompt v4; every attempt, accepted document and ledger entry carries prompt_version, validator_version, ask_words and insertion_mode.
- D-052 **Pilot v4 design and budget:** the same 30 ids plus 20 fresh stratified ids (4 each of employee email, meeting minutes, complaint letter + reply, training handbook page, policy manual section) in data/pilot_v4; per-prompt session cap $0.40 and pilot total under $1.00. The pre-start check compares an expected-cost projection (2 attempts per doc, output at the ask length) with the remaining cap because the all-worst-case projection (3 attempts, 700 tokens, every doc) is $0.7 and would never allow the run; every call still reserves its own worst case, so a cap breach stops the run mid-way.
